About

Researching the hidden choices in AI systems

I am a fifth-year PhD candidate in Information Systems at the University of Maryland’s Robert H. Smith School of Business, advised by Jessica Clark. I expect to complete my degree in May 2027.

My research treats data preprocessing, experimental search, and system design as consequential information-quality decisions. I examine how these choices interact with model tuning, affect group disparities, and shape the reliability of conclusions drawn from predictive and generative AI systems.

I also develop AI applications with direct social value, including an LLM-based system that supports educators in providing personalized math feedback to underserved students.

Job market paper

Measuring the Hidden Impact of Pipeline Choices: Data Preprocessing, Model Tuning, and Joint Search

Aseem Baji and Jessica Clark

This work develops a common framework for comparing experimental effort devoted to data preprocessing, model tuning, and joint pipeline search. It shows how conclusions about individual choices can change when the full pipeline is optimized together.
